Klik-pro simplifies your daily computer tasks. This application lives in your menu bar and gives you control over mouse shortcuts, app profiles, and system settings. You save time by creating custom actions for your mouse buttons. You also manage different app instances and system states with one click.

## 🛠️ What this app does

Modern computers require frequent context switching. You open browsers, toggle between tabs, and manage open windows. Klik-pro reduces the effort for these tasks.

### Mouse Button Shortcuts
You assign specific actions to your mouse buttons. If you use a mouse with extra side buttons, this app translates those clicks into shortcuts. You trigger complex workflows with a single press.

### Isolated App Profiles
The app creates isolated instances for specific software. If you use AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude, this feature separates these sessions from your main browser window. You keep your work and your research in distinct digital spaces.

### Tab Switching
Navigation becomes faster with the thumb-wheel tab switching feature. You scroll through open tabs without moving the mouse pointer to the top of the browser. This movement saves time during long research sessions.

### Keep-Awake Utility
The keep-awake menu prevents your computer from sleeping. You toggle this setting when you run long tasks or monitor background processes. Your screen remains active until you turn the feature off.

## ⚙️ Setting up your preferences

Once the app runs, you see the icon in your top menu bar. Click this icon to access the settings panel.

### Assigning shortcuts
Open the Shortcut Settings tab. Click a mouse button field and then press the button on your mouse. The app waits for your input. After you select the button, choose an action from the dropdown menu. You save your changes instantly.

### Managing profiles
To create a new app profile, click the Plus icon in the Profiles tab. Enter the name of the website or the application you want to isolate. The app creates a container for that specific profile. You launch this profile from the menu bar whenever you need it.

### Adjusting tab behavior
Check the box for Thumb-wheel integration to enable fast tab navigation. You test this by scrolling while your cursor hovers over your browser window. Adjust the sensitivity slider if the switching feels too fast or too slow.

## 📋 System requirements

Klik-pro runs on most modern Mac computers. Ensure your system meets these requirements:

- macOS 12.0 or newer.
- An active internet connection for initial setup.
- A mouse with at least three buttons for the best experience.
- Sufficient disk space for temporary app data.

If you encounter performance issues, check the Accessibility settings in your System Preferences. Ensure the app has permission to operate in the background.

## 💬 Frequently asked questions

Do I need a special mouse?
No. Standard mice with left, right, and middle buttons work fine. Extra side buttons offer more customization options.

Does this app monitor my activity?
No. The app processes all shortcuts locally on your machine. Your data stays on your hard drive.

Can I turn off the keep-awake feature automatically?
The app provides a manual toggle. You control when the system stays active.

What happens if I update my operating system?
The app updates regularly to stay compatible with new system versions. Check the GitHub page to see if a newer version is available.

## 💡 Troubleshooting tips

If your mouse shortcuts stop working, restart the app from the menu bar. Sometimes system updates reset the accessibility permissions. Navigate to System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and re-enable access for Klik-pro. Contact the support channel on GitHub if the problem persists.
